My story, in my own words.

I did not start in tech. I started in agribusiness. At Savoy Estate I handled the day to day running of a business, and at JR Farms I ran a factory floor. That is where I learned that a plan only matters if real people can carry it out.

Alongside that, I was learning the forex market, and I have now spent more than nine years in it. I study the chart, design strategies, and automate them, so rules make the call and emotion does not. Technical analysis taught me patience, risk discipline, and how to test an idea honestly.

I do not guess. I look at the evidence, build the plan, and test it.

I took that same habit into business analysis: research the market, check the feasibility, talk to the stakeholders, write it down clearly. I am now deepening it through the TechCrush Business Analysis program, and I work as a data annotator with Public AI, where accuracy is the whole job.

Then there is the community side. I founded The Nexus Lab in Ilorin to give young talent a real place to build. Today it has 23 members across 9 departments. I also serve as an ambassador for Haven1 and Sentz, explaining DeFi and everyday on and off ramps in plain language. All of it runs on one idea I call Visionary Pursuit.
